ARIZONA HEAT

Stay Hydrated

Arizona’s dry heat increases dehydration risk. Sip water throughout the day, even if you don’t feel thirsty. Carry a bottle when outdoors and check with your doctor if you have heart or kidney conditions.

MOVEMENT

Walk Every Day

A gentle 20–30 minute walk in the cooler morning or evening hours can support mobility, balance and heart health. Choose shaded paths or indoor malls when temperatures soar.

NUTRITION

Eat Balanced Meals

Focus on colorful fruits, vegetables, whole grains, beans and lean proteins. Arizona’s farmers markets offer great seasonal produce that supports energy and overall wellness.

PREVENTION

Schedule Regular Checkups

Routine visits help monitor blood pressure, vision, hearing, bone health and more. Stay up to date with recommended screenings and vaccinations based on your doctor’s guidance.

Why These Tips Matter in Arizona

🌡️ Extreme Heat

Summer temperatures demand extra attention to fluids and timing of outdoor activity.

🌵 Dry Climate

Low humidity means you lose moisture faster without noticing thirst.

☀️ Strong Sun

Sun protection and smart scheduling protect skin and energy levels.

🏞️ Active Lifestyle

Arizona offers beautiful outdoor spaces perfect for gentle daily movement.
